Why Build for Illinois?

More Than a Competition

Build for Illinois is designed not only as a competition, but as an intensive learning experience.

Competition + Training + Real-World Feedback
✓Can you identify a meaningful problem?
✓Can you understand real users and stakeholders?
✓Can you make decisions under constraints?
✓Can you work across disciplines?
✓Can you use mentor feedback effectively?
✓Can you turn emerging technology into a useful product?
✓Can you actually ship?

Students should leave with more than a prize or project. They should leave with a stronger ability to build products in an AI-driven world.

Built for the AI era

Training Builders for the AI Era

As AI lowers the cost of implementation, the ability to choose meaningful problems, understand users, navigate constraints, and make strong product decisions becomes even more important.

Problem selectionUser understandingJudgmentProduct thinkingSystem thinkingPrioritizationCommunicationIterationCross-disciplinary work
Building is getting easier.
Knowing what should be built is not.

Build for Illinois gives students a compressed environment to practice those skills with real feedback.

Mentors & Judges

Help Students Build Better Products

Build for Illinois depends on experienced people who can challenge assumptions, share real-world context, and help student teams make better decisions.

What happens after Sunday?

FAQ

Questions We Expect Students to Ask

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Teams are interdisciplinary, although each team needs enough technical capacity to build a functional product or prototype.

Do I need a team before the event?

No. Team formation support will be available.

Who can participate?

UIUC undergraduate and master’s students across majors.

How large is the event?

Approximately 50–100 participants, intentionally capped to preserve mentor access.

Is this just a coding hackathon?

No. Product thinking, user understanding, iteration, execution, and real-world impact are central to the event.

Will Illinois App adopt winning projects?

No. No adoption is guaranteed. Some promising projects may receive further review or stakeholder feedback.

Where will the event take place?

The current working venue is the iSchool Hub, 4th Floor, pending final confirmation.
