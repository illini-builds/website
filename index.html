The competition

Build it. Improve it. Launch it.

Solve a problem you notice at Illinois, or pick one from our Real Problem Bank. You'll build an MVP during the weekend, improve it after feedback, and present it as a product launch—not a code walkthrough.

The iSchool difference: Judges focus on the elegance of your problem-solving, user understanding, product value, and execution—not lines of code. A demonstrable product and evidence of at least one iteration are required for award eligibility.

Rules & judging

Eligibility & requirements

3–5 per teamAll majorsUIUC undergraduates & master's studentsAI tools permitted

Bring your own campus problem or use our Real Problem Bank. Competition development must occur during the event weekend; existing libraries, APIs and frameworks are permitted. Teams must show at least one meaningful, documented iteration. Without it, a team is ineligible for awards.

Product-first evaluation

Each judge scores independently; scores are averaged. Proposed criteria, subject to final review:

Problem & users25%
Product value & impact25%
Working MVP & execution25%
Iteration & decisions15%
Product launch10%

Frequently asked questions

Who can participate?

UIUC undergraduate and master's students from all majors. Teams are expected to have 3–5 members.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Interdisciplinary teams are welcome, although the team must deliver a demonstrable MVP.

Do I need a project idea?

No. Bring a problem from your own campus experience or select a challenge from the Real Problem Bank.

Can I use AI tools?

Yes. AI tools, public APIs and libraries are permitted. Teams remain responsible for their decisions, product and submitted work.

What do judges care about?

The product launch is about the clarity, value and elegance of your solution, not a code walkthrough. A meaningful documented iteration is required for award eligibility.

Will meals be provided?

Yes. Friday features a Chinese New Year–themed dinner; Saturday includes breakfast, lunch and dinner; Sunday includes breakfast and lunch. Exact logistics remain subject to confirmation.

Is the venue confirmed?

The planned location is iSchool Hub, 4th and 5th floors. Final venue access and logistics will be communicated to participants.

Who should I contact?

Event questions: yunhuang@illinois.edu. Sponsorship: contact@uiuccesa.com.
