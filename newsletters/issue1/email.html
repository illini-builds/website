Build for Illinois 2027 is happening!
We're excited to introduce Build for Illinois 2027, a student-centered product hackathon co-organized by the Chinese Engineering Students Association (CESA) and the School of Information Sciences at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
Our mission is simple: bring students together to solve real campus challenges and turn creative ideas into working products.
Build it. Improve it. Launch it.
Save the Date!
January 29–31, 2027
iSchool Hub · 4th Floor · UIUC
Open to undergraduate and master's students from all majors. No previous hackathon experience required!
Not Just Another Hackathon!
We're putting mentorship and iteration at the heart of the experience.
Every team will work with mentors through at least two structured feedback sessions, improving their product through two rounds of iteration before the final demo.
It's not just about what you build. It's about how you make it better.
What Will You Build?
By the end of the weekend, each team will submit:
•Working MVP: A demonstrable product that solves a real problem.
•Demo Video: Show your product in action.
•Five-Slide Deck: Present your problem, solution, MVP, iteration, impact, and next steps.
•GitHub + Reports: Share your repository and document your mentor sessions.
And there's more! Participants who successfully complete the event will receive a digital Certificate of Completion that can be added to their LinkedIn profiles.
Your Adventure Starts Here!
We're preparing an exciting weekend of building, learning, and connecting with fellow Illinois students.
