Build for Illinois 2027 is legit now!
We're excited to introduce Build for Illinois 2027, a student-centered hackathon co-organized by the Chinese Engineering Students Association (CESA) and the School of Information Sciences at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
Our mission is simple: bring students together to identify real challenges, develop creative solutions, and turn ideas into meaningful products.
Build it. Improve it. Launch it.
Meet Our Faculty Advisor: Professor Yun Huang
Read more about MapInsights at the iSchool
 
Meet Professor Yun Huang, Associate Professor at the School of Information Sciences and Co-Director of the Social Computing Systems (SALT) Lab.
Her research focuses on human-computer interaction, social computing, and human-AI collaboration, exploring how technology can better serve people and communities.
One recent example is MapInsights, an interactive mapping platform developed by Professor Huang to help campus public safety personnel learn the geography of Illinois more effectively. The platform transforms traditional memorization into interactive, personalized training.
It's a great example of how a real campus challenge can inspire a practical technological solution—the very spirit behind Build for Illinois!
